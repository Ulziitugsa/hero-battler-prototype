# Character appeal and revised art direction

Latest update: the user approved the cute/cool Moonwater pixel-art designs and requested chunkier pixels and animation. `PIXEL-PROTOTYPE.md` is now the current visual brief; the anime research below remains context for character appeal.

## User feedback takes precedence

The user rejected the generated Royal Guard and Battle Captain portraits: they look too obviously AI-generated. They are not registered in the game's art manifest. Those studies are stored under `docs/art-studies`, outside the shipped public assets.

The user supplied an anime-style Runihara card as the desired visual reference. Apply its design qualities, not its character, costume, composition, symbols, rules, or frame. This supersedes the woodcut/gouache proposal as the target for future roster production. The three previously integrated legendary studies are provisional, not approval for a full roster in that style.

Target: expressive anime faces; exaggerated readable proportions; strong dark contour lines; clear local colors; deliberate cel shadows; memorable hair/ear/weapon silhouettes; playful or heroic poses. Limit material detail. Avoid glossy pseudo-realism, ornate gold armor everywhere, airbrushed faces, washed-out brown palettes, and decorative complexity that replaces character design.

The first reference-led test is `../art-studies/flame-imp-anime-concept.png`. It is a concept, not a shipped asset. Its upper ear is clipped and some surfaces are still overly textured. Fix composition and simplify texture before promotion. Do not batch-produce the roster until the visual direction is confirmed through feedback.

## What the commercial evidence supports

[Sensor Tower's May 2022 report](https://sensortower.com/blog/genshin-impact-three-billion-revenue) estimated Genshin Impact exceeded $3 billion in App Store/Google Play spending and ranked first among gacha-based mobile games in Q1 2022. That establishes historical commercial success, not a ranking of individual character designs or a claim about 2026 leadership.

[Sensor Tower's US Q4 2025 summary](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-3d-anime-games-revenue-us-600e1f92241bc16eb85fd74c) includes Love and Deepspace, Genshin Impact, and Honkai: Star Rail, with substantial weekly revenue peaks. It is a US mobile quarterly snapshot, published in its AI Insights series; it is not worldwide audited revenue or individual-character attribution.

[AUTOMATON's May 2026 NIKKE report](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/goddess-of-victory-nikke-lifetime-revenue-tops-1-3-billion-with-japanese-players-accounting-for-the-biggest-share-by-far/) describes revenue increases around anniversary character releases alongside a major advertising campaign. This illustrates the confounding factors: character identity, power, accumulated story attachment, offers, and marketing change together. It cannot isolate visual attractiveness as the cause.

No dependable public dataset examined here establishes which isolated character appearance sells the most. Do not turn fan banner charts, usage rates, or whole-game revenue estimates into that claim. The user's specific visual preference is stronger guidance for this project than a guessed universal beauty formula.

## Roster direction: a hypothesis to test

Build variety inside one drawing language: attractive adult male and female heroes, cute mischievous creatures, elegant mysterious characters, and epic armored or monstrous champions. The latter can appeal through power and personality without conventional beauty. Do not make every character grim, elderly, scarred, or skeletal; equally, do not reduce every design to the same attractive face with different hair.

Every character brief must answer: who wants this character, what is immediately appealing, what makes the silhouette recognizable, what personality the pose expresses, and what memorable battle moment supports the fantasy. Rarity alone is not an identity.

Initial existing-card candidates: Royal Guard (charismatic protector), Battle Captain (confident aspirational commander), Flame Imp (cute troublemaker), Mira (elegant mysterious guide), Hellhound (sleek ferocious companion). Preserve existing card IDs and mechanics while exploring art.

## Preference test before production

Show 8–12 concepts in random order, at the actual card size, without rarity, price, or stats. Ask target players which three they would most want to collect, which they would ignore, and why. Then reveal short personalities and ask again. Test mechanics separately so overpowered cards do not masquerade as successful visual design. Record audience differences and reasons; a small playtest is directional evidence, not a sales forecast. No real-money spending test is needed at this stage.

Separate an appealing drawing from a production-ready asset: check hands, face consistency, silhouette, cropped features, effects, thumbnail readability, and agreement with the rest of the roster. Keep source prompts and rejected studies so the same mistake is not repeated.

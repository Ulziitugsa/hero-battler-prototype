# Integrated Moonwater assets

Additional sets: [expanded characters and exact prompts](PIXEL-EXPANSION-PROMPTS.md), [spell illustrations and exact prompts](PIXEL-SPELL-PROMPTS.md), and [verification / remaining work](MOONWATER-QA.md). Final images are saved under `public/art/pixel/` in the game workspace.

Built-in image generation was used for the project-bound raster art. Assets live in `public/art/pixel/`: three faction atlases, three eight-frame idle sheets, and `ashen-road.png`. Expansion prompts are in PIXEL-EXPANSION-PROMPTS.md. The renderer uses 160 × 160 pixels after user feedback that 96 × 96 obscured facial detail. Eight idle frames advance at 125 ms intervals. Higher-detail original files remain intact.

## gamePixelAnimationPrompt

Use this approved character as an identity reference. Create a NEW production animation spritesheet of this EXACT SAME character and setting, EIGHT frames arranged exactly 4 columns x 2 rows, each frame SQUARE, no gutters, no text. Landscape output 2048x1024. Authentic chunky pixel art, each frame visually only 80x80 logical pixels enlarged nearest-neighbor. Extremely clear large pixel clusters, 24 flat colors, absolutely NO fine textures, no smooth gradients, no tiny detail. Preserve recognisable silhouette and cute/cool appeal. Same fixed camera, same exact unchanging background in all 8 panels, character feet and head registered exactly. Smooth small cyclic idle motion progresses across eight consecutive frames, cloth or tail waves gently in one direction then returns. Only one quick blink in frame 5 (guardian instead teal rune pulse). No change of pose, size, angle, perspective or location. Subtle breathing, don't jump around. It is essential that first and last frames join seamlessly.

## gamePixelCampaignPrompt

Production background for Embervale pixel art campaign map, landscape 1536x1024. Same Moonwater world: crisp chunky 16-bit pixel art drawn on approximately 384x256 logical grid and nearest-neighbor enlarged. Navy twilight and warm amber lanterns, teal woods, distant violet mountains. A winding dirt road travels left to right across the entire scene, starting at tiny lantern-lit village on left, crossing a river by a stone bridge in middle, ending at ancient ruined castle on a hill at right. Broad readable shapes and quiet flat space for map nodes. Environment only, no text, no UI, no characters or markers. Charming adventurous storybook mood. No intricate texture or painterly effects.

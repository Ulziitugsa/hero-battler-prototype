New original design: a small mischievous soot-black fire imp, a creature rather than a human, with enormous amber eyes and cream muzzle, two long pointed ears (one folded), a short wedge-shaped orange flame tuft, tiny batlike charcoal wings and a curling arrow tail. It wears an oversized brick-red messenger scarf tied with one round brass button and carries a battered little kettle that contains its fire. Its expression is a cocky delighted grin with one small fang, like a tiny troublemaker caught stealing a coal. Dynamic mid-hop diagonal pose, one paw held up with a single little angular yellow flame above it, kettle held by other paw; three clearly drawn fingers per paw. Head and ears entirely inside picture, face occupies a large clear area of upper half. Background a few broad cream and warm terracotta furnace shapes, minimal secondary detail. Drawing execution is essential: crisp varied-weight dark brown outlines, flat local colors, just two hard-edged shadow tones per material, selectively softened flame edge only. Saturated orange and amber focal points with charcoal and cream contrast. Appealing 2D anime creature collectible card illustration with an intentionally designed silhouette; simplified chunky shapes and clear readable paws. Avoid all photographic lighting, realistic skin, glossy 3D, metallic armor, painterly brush texture, antique paper texture, crosshatching, ornament, particles, airbrushed gradients, bloom and hyperdetailed fantasy concept art. Not a plush toy render. Prioritize a charming hand-drawn animation-cel feeling and expression.
